From “what if” to “like this.”

  1. Add C1 and put them where their walk starts.
  2. Press Record movement, drag C1 to walk in, then press Stop recording.
  3. Add or pick C2, then press Record movement again. C1’s recording plays from the beginning.
  4. Wait until C1 finishes. Then drag the faded C2 into the scene and press Stop recording. C2 stays hidden in the replay until you first moved them.
  5. Press ▶ Rewatch to see C1 and C2 act together. Repeat for more characters!
